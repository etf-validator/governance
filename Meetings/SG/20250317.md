## Updates from ii

- The development of the new test driver based on a DSL for the German National Mapping Agency progressed and is almost ready to be moved to production. It allows to develop tests for both web services and data/metadata, overcoming the limitations of SoapUI and XQuery, respectively. 
- Once finalised, it will be included in a new ETF version.

## Updates from JRC

- Validator
  - The latest version ([v.2024.3](https://github.com/INSPIRE-MIF/helpdesk-validator/releases/tag/v2024.3)) is still the one released in September 2024 and used in the 2024 INSPIRE Monitoring and Reporting to validate all Member States metadata (approximately 350k in total) - see the related [dashboard](https://inspire-geoportal.ec.europa.eu/mr/mr2024.html).
- Starting in 2025, a new [release plan](https://github.com/INSPIRE-MIF/helpdesk-validator/tree/master/release%20strategy) was produced, which only foresees one release per year happening in June. This will only include bugfixes and security fixes, without new functionality.
- ETF
  - The new version (v.17) of the [OSGeoLive](http://live.osgeo.org/en/index.html), which will also include ETF, has still to be published. 
- INSPIRE
  - The GreenData4All initiative, which aims to revise the INSPIRE Directive, is progressing. A [public consultation](https://ec.europa.eu/info/law/better-regulation/have-your-say/initiatives/13170-GreenData4All-updated-rules-on-geospatial-environmental-data-and-access-to-environmental-information_en) is open until 28 April 2025.
  - The [INSPIRE Maintenance and Implementation Work Programme 2025-26](https://wikis.ec.europa.eu/display/InspireMIG/INSPIRE+work+programme+2025-26) was endorsed by the MIG. It includes the maintenance of the current infrastructure (Geoportal, Validator, Registry, schemas, TGs, UML models) until the future legal framework will be in place.
